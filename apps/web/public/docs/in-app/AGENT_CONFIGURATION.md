# Agent Configuration

Agents are the intelligent components that Optifiner uses to evolve your code. They analyze your codebase, apply transformations guided by your fitness metrics, and iteratively improve functionality. This guide explores the different types of agents available in Optifiner—from language-specific analyzers to general-purpose transformers—and shows you how to configure them for your specific optimization goals. Understanding agent types and their capabilities is crucial for directing evolution toward the improvements that matter most to your project.

In this section, you'll learn how to select the right agents for your codebase, configure their behavior through parameters and settings, and monitor their performance during evolution runs. We'll also cover common configuration patterns for different scenarios—whether you're optimizing for performance, refactoring legacy code, or exploring novel implementations—and help you troubleshoot issues when agents aren't producing the results you expect.
